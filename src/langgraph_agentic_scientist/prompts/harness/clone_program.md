Trusted clone of a candidate program: create `to_dir` (`program_<idx>`) from `from_dir` (`program_<idx-1>`) by applying the source's `program_spec.toml` copy rules, without carrying evaluator artifacts. Paths are relative to your working directory. For every source after `program_0` the clone is refused until canonical evaluation and mirrored rubric artifacts prove the previous iteration is complete, and when the source already passes all rubrics. The tool reports what it copied, or the reason it refused.
